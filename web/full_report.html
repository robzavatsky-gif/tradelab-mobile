Research constraints and important limitations

All strategies are long-only, intraday, simulated fractional shares; maximum $250 notional, 50% of starting segment equity, 3 bps slippage per side unless otherwise indicated, no commissions, no real spread or impact, and no hard guaranteed loss cap. Base stops 0.5%, targets 1%; adaptive exit is bounded at 0.35–0.8% stop with target 1.5× stop; 90-minute variant exits at the open of the relevant later bar. If both stop and target touch in the same 5-minute candle, stop is assumed first. No margin, options, shorts, broker order routing or money movement.

Gate: only allow signals at/after 10:30 when first-hour range is below 0.75% and at least 0.25%, first-hour net direction exceeds 40% of that range, signal close is above approximate IEX intraday VWAP, and signal volume is at least 60% of the opening hour median. These thresholds are research hypotheses, not proven edges. The baseline strategies are kept for comparison. Opening-hour regime is only an approximate classification, not knowledge of the day's realized trend.

Only IEX data is used. The filtered price/volume and exits may not correspond to real-world fills. Regimes, signals and decisions are logged in per-day diagnostics CSV. Existing known historical data is for research; do not call it an untouched test. Prospective scores must be allowed to include losses without changing frozen rules.
